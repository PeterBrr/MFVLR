## Exact production boundary

For approved data only, the intended call sequence is `with_language(images, unshifted_ids, sample_ids=ordered_ids)` followed by `compute_objectives(predictions, SuppliedTargets(...))`. The latter enforces target/prediction IDs and exact token equality and retains FD/LR/CMC/FL/AR/KL definitions. No user-facing trainer should reconstruct these losses independently. D02B10 `integration_contract.py` checks synthetic metadata/tensors and mock method calls; it does not claim to validate tokenizer semantics or the real graph.

The frozen full model owns 154,156,232 parameters. Physical visual deployment owns 41,135,303 parameters according to `integration.ownership_report`; it excludes FLT, language adapter and CMC temperature. These are derived implementation counts, not recovered author details or evidence of model quality.

## Numerical readiness limitation

Original G01 isolated LR is 248,989.578125 with all 308 tokens; total 249,028.046875. Historical isolated LR gradient norm is about 37.14 million, while B1 CMC is zero and the real fixture has no manipulated foreground. These are preserved G01 observations from D02B7-R2/D02B8, not new training results. They limit extrapolation to multi-sample/fake data. Finite loss, finite gradients, additivity agreement, finite optimizer updates, repeated-step stability, convergence and benchmark accuracy are distinct gates.

No root cause for the additivity failure is established. The same-forward experiment is a bounded diagnostic, not a prerequisite to unlimited CPU audits. Its result should lead to an explicit next-step decision while keeping all historical failures intact.
