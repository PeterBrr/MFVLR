# D02B11 dataset decision

**Recommend a small FaceForensics++ original/Face2Face pilot.** This is a proposed dataset choice, not download, preprocessing or training authorization. It offers supplied manipulation masks and a connection to MFVLR's FF++ evaluation. No candidate below is already a verified, subject-disjoint, aligned 224x224 input package.

Official sources checked 2026-10-17. “Accessible” means a published official access route; no account permission, archive contents or successful download is claimed.

## Concrete proposed pilot: FF++-F2F-1200

All choices in this paragraph are **PROPOSED engineering choices**, not paper requirements or permission to process data:

- Original versus **Face2Face only**, c23 videos and the publisher's corresponding masks. Prefer Face2Face over Deepfakes' rectangular Poisson-edit masks or NeuralTextures' tracking-region mask caveat for this first localization pilot; this is a target-clarity choice, not a quality ranking. [Official mask explanations](https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics/blob/master/dataset/README.md#Masks).
- Target **60 original/manipulated clip pairs**, with 40/10/10 target pairs from official train/validation/test partitions; **10 matched frame positions per pair**. Result: 800/200/200 images, balanced Real/Fake in each partition, 1,200 total. Select no frame IDs until the extraction protocol is approved.
- Require subject and source/driver connected groups to be disjoint across partitions. Keep all derivatives in their source group. Audit identities rather than equating video IDs with people. Exclude cross-partition conflicts; if quotas cannot be met without leakage, stop and report the shortfall rather than relaxing separation.
- Deliverable input remains float32 RGB `[3,224,224]` in [0,1], int64 binary `[224,224]` masks, class 0=Real / 1=Fake. An approved protocol must define synchronized frame selection, crop/alignment and mask transforms. Do not threshold image differences or create fake masks. Encoding an all-background target for verified real images still needs explicit dataset-protocol approval under the current no-mask-synthesis restriction.
- Budget **1.12 GiB** for the final 1,200 RGB+int64-mask tensors alone (derived from shapes/dtypes); encoded source/mask downloads, crops and caches are additional and not measured. Acquire only the selected clips/masks after access approval; do not fetch the entire raw-image collection.

**Language blocker to resolve with this dataset decision:** Face2Face is absent from the current approved generator registry. Do not alias it to Diffae, FaceSwapper or a GAN. MFVLR uses only L1/L2 for its FF++ training (Section IV-B, p.10), whereas the accepted executable baseline packs four 77-token segments. Approve an explicit FF++ prompt/segment policy separately; do not invent L3/L4, silently repeat segments or change sequence length. The dataset is recommended, but six-loss training is not ready until that policy is resolved.

## What can be compared with MFVLR?

MFVLR Table VIII (p.13) includes FF++ detection ACC/AUC, including FF++->FF++ 96.37/99.69, but reports no FF++ localization score. Its GenFace diffusion/localization results use other generators and a source-pair mask procedure (Section III-E, pp.7–8).

**No result from this 1,200-image, one-manipulation pilot is directly numerically comparable to the paper.** It can establish dataset-to-detector/localizer integration and report clearly labeled internal detection/localization measurements after authorization. Eventual FF++ detection comparison requires matching the paper's training/data/compression/frame/prompt/evaluation protocol; those details are not all recovered. Pilot localization with publisher masks cannot be claimed as reproduction of GenFace mIoU or diffusion generalization. [Existing paper traceability](paper_analysis.md).

## Decision requested

Approve **FF++ original/Face2Face as the pilot data source and the proposed 1,200-image scope**, subject to official access, subject audit, reviewed preprocessing/mask handling and explicit FF++ prompt policy. This does not authorize any download, processing, model execution, optimizer or benchmark now. No dataset was downloaded, no mask synthesized and no image preprocessed during D02B11.
